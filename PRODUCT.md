# OneForAll — Product context

Inferred from `docs/diseno/specs/2026-11-01-dj-app-design.md` and the owner's brief (no separate interview: the owner asked to keep design iterations lean).

- **What:** Windows desktop app (Mac later) to search YouTube Music, download tracks and playlists, convert formats, and manage a DJ library. Later phases: BPM/key, stems, audio tools, discovery.
- **Who:** the owner, a hobbyist DJ, plus DJs like them. Spanish-speaking; all UI copy in Spanish.
- **Usage scene:** at a desk preparing sets; many searches and downloads in a row; long-running jobs in the background while browsing.
- **Success:** find the right song fast (no junk results), download in the chosen format with correct tags, trust that cancel and duplicates behave.
- **Surface mode:** Operate (app UI). Scanability and native feel over expression.
- **Pinned aesthetic:** Apple Liquid Glass (`Liquid_Glass.md`): translucent, refractive, clean, sharp. Dark by default, Windows 11 acrylic window, system blue accent, Inter + Plus Jakarta Sans.
- **Anti-references:** the previous app's cluttered results and silent failures (see `review.txt`).
